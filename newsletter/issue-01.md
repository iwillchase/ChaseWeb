## Games

![Lost Hats](https://iwillchase.github.io/ChaseWeb/img/LostHatsThumbnail.gif)

Three small games you can play in your browser for free:

- **[Naga in the Colosseum](https://cute-grub.itch.io/naga-in-the-colloseum)**: a snake fighting for its life in an arena, made with Jay.
- **[Lost Hats](https://cute-grub.itch.io/lost-hats)**: a fun puzzle game. Shift and change the level to build a sort of hat-guiding machine, and get the hats to the door. Made with friends, Joe and Jay.
- **[Dream Cycle](https://cute-grub.itch.io/dream-cycle)**: ride a bike through the sky, through your dream. Be careful: nightmares are dreams too!

[See the games on my site →](https://iwillchase.github.io/ChaseWeb/?ref=newsletter-1#games)
