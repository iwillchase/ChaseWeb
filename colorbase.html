ColorBase

A number system made of colors. It counts in base 8, and each digit is one of the 8 colors of light. Its three bits are red, green, and blue, and each bit adds one line to the digit's symbol.

Keyboard works too: 0–7, + − * /, Enter, Backspace, Esc.

The digits

Places

Each place up adds an ending, the way "ten" and "hundred" do. Reading 12 in ColorBase gives Red-er Green (one 8 and two 1s, or 10). Blacks are said too, because they change the color: 7 is White, but 07 is Black-er White, a darker gray.
